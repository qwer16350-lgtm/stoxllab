# STOXL Hermes Agent Organization README

## Organization Purpose

STOXL Hermes Discord Agent Organization is a planned Discord-based working structure for marketing, operation, and strategy support. Its current state is design/configuration/dry-run/documentation. It is not a live Discord deployment.

The organization is designed to keep external-facing work careful: juniors prepare drafts or research, seniors review and report, and decision makers approve. External execution remains human-only.

## Decision Makers

Final decision makers:

- 김태호_STOXL
- 이주호_STOXL

No agent has final approval authority.

## Departments

### 마케팅팀

Purpose: SNS, homepage, brand communication, and marketing strategy.

Members:

- 루시: senior reviewer
- 마린: junior draft/research agent

Flow:

- 마린 -> 루시 -> 결정권자

### 운영팀

Members:

- 카스미: junior research/condition capture agent

Flow:

- 카스미 -> 메이코 -> 결정권자

Purpose: brand interpretation, product direction, business direction, exhibition/pop-up direction, and strategic critique.

Members:

- 레제: independent strategy agent

Flow:

- 레제 -> 결정권자

Reze may give strategic opinions to the marketing and operation teams, but cannot directly command practical work.

## Agents

### 루시

Lucy is the marketing senior. She reviews Marin's drafts and research, manages STOXL's brand tone, and prepares approval-needed marketing reports.

Cannot:

- Publish SNS without approval
- Apply homepage changes without approval
- Confirm official brand direction alone

### 마린

Marin is the marketing junior. She creates SNS drafts, homepage copy drafts, content ideas, and reference research.

Cannot:

- Finalize publish copy
- Present drafts as final
- Bypass Lucy review

### 메이코

Meiko is the operation senior. She reviews Kasumi's research and judges whether competitions or grants should be recommended, held, or rejected.

Cannot:

- Submit applications without approval
- Contact external organizations without approval
- Confirm contract-like conditions

### 카스미

Kasumi is the operation junior. She searches for competitions, grants, exhibitions, pop-ups, and external opportunities, then organizes conditions and uncertainty.

Cannot:

- Finalize support recommendations
- Treat uncertain information as confirmed
- Bypass Meiko review

### 레제

Reze is the independent strategy office agent. She reviews STOXL's brand, product, working folders, and RAG context to suggest product, business, exhibition, and pop-up directions.

Cannot:

- Directly command practical teams
- Override Lucy or Meiko's final judgment
- Confirm official direction without decision maker approval

## Approval And External Execution

Agents can research, draft, review, and report according to their roles. They cannot approve final execution.

Approval-required items include:

- SNS publish
- Homepage upload
- Competition/grant submission
- External email send
- Price, contract, and delivery schedule confirmation
- Official brand direction confirmation
- External collaboration condition confirmation

Even after approval, external execution is human-only. Bots do not post, submit, email, upload, or confirm contract-like terms.
